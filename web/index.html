Quick Start

Run The Lab

Use Sim to pause, step, reset, change speed, scrub time, and restore a timeline frame as the live simulation.

Add Bodies

Use Add, choose a kind, then click-drag for manual velocity. Turn on Rest to drop a body with zero velocity, or Orbit to compute an orbital start around the selected target.

Inspect

Click or hover a body for live values. The Inspect tab edits name, color, mass, position, velocity, radius, trails, cloning, freezing, and deletion.

Mouse And Keyboard

Mouse

Wheel zooms around the cursor. Drag empty space to pan. Click a body to focus. Double-click opens Inspect. In Track pair mode, click two bodies to measure distance.

Keys

H hides or shows the HUD. F fits the system. R resets the view. Period steps once. Question mark opens Help.

Physics Readouts

dt

The physics timestep: how much simulated time each integration step covers. Smaller dt is safer for close encounters but slower.

Energy Drift

A health check. Low drift means the numerical orbit is staying physically consistent. High drift usually means close encounters or a timestep that is too large.

Vectors

Velocity shows motion direction. Accel shows net gravitational acceleration. Forces shows the total summed force. Pull to lock shows only the pull from the locked body.

COM

The center of mass. In isolated systems it is the mass-weighted balance point of all bodies.
